## Honest gaps (grounded in repo facts)
- The facts establish hash-chained receipts and offline recomputation, but do not establish cryptographic signing, keyless identity, SLSA provenance, or in-toto predicates; a modified chain and its terminal hash could otherwise be regenerated by whoever controls the run.
- The README says the action installs szl-ci-witness at an exact reviewed commit, but the supplied facts do not provide the actual wrapped commit, action.yml, installer, or verification of that pin, so pin enforcement and install integrity cannot be audited here.
- The usage example pins checkout and setup-python by full SHA, while the witness step contains a <40-char-sha> placeholder; the released usage and tag-annotation process therefore remain unverified from the facts.
- The example restores the previous chain through actions:read, but the supplied README head does not show the artifact download/upload steps, retention behavior, first-run initialization, or how the correct previous artifact is authenticated.
- Concurrency is configured per workflow and ref, but the facts do not establish cross-branch fork/merge semantics, protection against parallel artifact writers, or recovery from missing and conflicting chain heads.
- The facts promise measured JUnit counts and deliberately reject success with a missing or failing report, but do not establish parser handling for skipped tests, errors, multiple suites, malformed XML, empty reports, or report substitution.
- No independent verifier command, receipt schema version, canonical JSON/hash algorithm, compatibility policy, or test corpus is present in the supplied facts, so the strength and portability of offline verification remain unproven.
- The action records failures, but the facts do not establish tamper-evident publication outside the workflow, artifact retention, release signing, or a transparency mechanism for long-term evidence custody.

## Dosed upgrade plan
- Dose 1 — specify before expanding: publish the receipt schema, canonicalization and hash rules, chain-head selection, branch semantics, and a precise threat model; state explicitly that receipts are not SLSA provenance or signed attestations.
- Dose 2 — make verification reproducible: propose a small offline verifier and golden/adversarial fixtures covering valid chains, red runs, missing JUnit XML, malformed XML, count changes, reordered entries, truncation, and terminal-hash mismatches; require failure fixtures to fail closed.
- Dose 3 — bind evidence exactly: record the JUnit report digest and stable run identifiers, validate conclusion against measured outcomes, and define artifact naming plus previous-head restoration and upload examples using least-privilege permissions.
- Dose 4 — harden the wrapper: inspect and test action.yml and the installer, pin szl-ci-witness by full reviewed commit, verify what was installed, pin all action dependencies, and replace the documentation placeholder only at a reviewed release.
- Dose 5 — add interoperability optionally: emit an in-toto/SLSA-compatible predicate or signed attestation for releases and evidence where supported, keeping unsigned offline verification available and documenting plan-dependent signature storage.
- Dose 6 — validate operations: exercise first run, repeated runs, red-to-green fixes, green-to-red regressions, canceled tests, artifact absence, retention expiry, and competing writers; add Scorecard only after its findings and maintenance cost are reviewed.
- Risk note: Medium-to-high assurance risk if the chain is presented as stronger than its evidence: the current facts do not prove signer identity, builder isolation, artifact completeness, or independent custody. Schema or hash changes can also invalidate existing chains, so changes require versioning, migration fixtures, and backward-compatible verification; external signing adds OIDC, availability, retention, and plan-limit dependencies.
- Propose-only note: This is a proposal based only on the supplied facts, not a claim that these files or controls already exist. Inspect the full repository and the pinned szl-ci-witness source before changing behavior; preserve failure recording and offline verification, and make signing or SLSA integration opt-in rather than silently redefining existing receipts.
